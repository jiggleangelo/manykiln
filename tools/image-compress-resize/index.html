Limits

Targets and quality are estimates. Some outputs grow. Formats depend on browser canvas support; AVIF encoding is unavailable here. GIF animation becomes one frame. JPEG uses a white background for transparency. One image at a time, up to 30 MiB and 40 megapixels. PNG encoding preserves the resized pixels; resizing itself changes the image.
