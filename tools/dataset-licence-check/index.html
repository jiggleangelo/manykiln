Limits and rules
Conservative policy checks on your declared JSON, not legal advice. It does not read licence text, verify anything externally, use an AI model or give legal clearance. Missing/UNKNOWN/NC licences, stale or missing review times, cycles and missing parents make the check fail. Only root ancestry receives licence checks; disconnected nodes are labelled unevaluated, while topology is checked across every component. Acknowledging obligations does not prove compliance. Supported policy names and conditions appear below. Actual terms, rights, use, jurisdiction and model or dataset restrictions require separate review.
Supported conservative policy
CC0-1.0: no acknowledgement. MIT, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause: notice. Apache-2.0: notice and apache-conditions; target must be Apache-2.0 or CC-BY-SA-4.0. CC-BY-4.0: attribution. CC-BY-SA-4.0: attribution and sharealike; target must match. All other identifiers fail the check. These are deliberately limited policy rules, not a complete licence compatibility matrix.
