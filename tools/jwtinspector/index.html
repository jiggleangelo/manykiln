Token and verification policy

Three-segment JWTs only. Paste test tokens; claims and keys remain in this tab's memory.

Untrusted decoded claims

Decoding is not authentication. A valid HMAC does not authorize access. This linter cannot verify RS256, ES256, JWKS, JWE or application policy.
