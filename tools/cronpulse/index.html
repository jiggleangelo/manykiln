Numeric wildcards, comma lists and ascending ranges. Steps such as */15 and Sunday 7 are explicit common extensions.
  • Both day fields restricted? Either may match (OR).
  • Spring gaps are skipped; repeated fall times produce both instants.
  • Start is exclusive. All output instants appear in three timezones.
  • Overlap means intersecting half-open duration intervals. Touching endpoints do not overlap.
Limits & trust boundary
No daemon jobs run. Search for next runs ends after 2,928 local dates (about eight years). An incomplete search is labelled. Overlap examines only the selected elapsed window, including jobs already active at its start. Dense windows exceeding 20,000 starts per schedule or 2,000 overlaps are rejected. IANA rules come from your browser and may differ from your scheduler. Unsupported macros, names, seconds, L/W/#/? and wraparound ranges are rejected. */n is treated as restricted in day fields; only a bare * is unrestricted.
